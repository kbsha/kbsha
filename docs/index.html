SEALED WITH CARE

A Letter for Your Heart

Dear Yorki,

If I could give you something more lasting than a gift, I would wish you the kind of gentleness that stays with you after the candles go out. I hope you meet people who listen to understand you, places that let you breathe, and little moments that remind you life can still surprise you beautifully.

I hope you never make yourself smaller to fit into someone else's idea of who you should be. Keep your questions, your books, your art, and your wonderfully thoughtful way of seeing things. The world needs people who look a little closer—and you have that gift.

And when life feels heavy, I hope you remember this: you are allowed to rest. You are allowed to be uncertain. You are allowed to receive care without having to earn it by being perfect, cheerful, or strong every minute.

I may not always find the perfect words, but I want my words to bring you warmth, never pressure. I hope this little corner of the internet makes you smile and reminds you that someone took time to make something just for you.

You deserve soft mornings, peaceful evenings, books that feel like old friends, art that understands your silence, and laughter that arrives when you least expect it.

You matter—not just for what you do, but for who you are. Your thoughts, your kindness, your little habits, and the way you experience the world are all part of what makes you wonderfully you.

May this new year of your life be full of gentle surprises. May you find courage for the things that scare you, patience for the things that take time, and enough peace to enjoy the beautiful things already around you.

With warmth, and a little stardust ✧

You do not have to be extraordinary every day to be precious. Even on your quietest days, you are still someone worth checking on, listening to, and treating gently. You deserve care that feels safe, kindness that asks for nothing in return, and room to be fully yourself. ♡
